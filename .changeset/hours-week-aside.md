The hours week view now shows **This week** and **Balances** beside the day table (below it on a narrower window). This week gives credited time against the target and the week's flexi as you type, and holds the "Convert this week's excess to TOIL and overtime" switch: switch it on to see the TOIL and overtime it makes, as a preview until the week's last working day and applied from then; the table's Converted column follows the switch. Balances shows your flexi, this month's TOIL against its cap, this year's overtime and the leave left. If you change a day after its week has been converted, a short message names what was recalculated ("Week of 5 Oct recalculated: TOIL 3:00 → 2:00, overtime 0:00 → 0:00", and "September totals changed" when an ended month moves). The week view's header also links to the summary for the week's month and to settings.
