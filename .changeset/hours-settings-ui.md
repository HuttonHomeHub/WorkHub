Add the hours tracker's settings screens at `/hours/settings`: set your working terms from a Monday (flexi targets and warning-only minimums per weekday, breaks, the working band, paid overtime and caps), set up leave years with a bought-leave switch, record opening balances and confirmed forfeits, and add a year's England and Wales bank holidays in one step. Deletes can be undone for 8 seconds. Times and durations are typed and shown as `h:mm`. The Hours tool joins the sidebar in a later release; until then open the page by its address.
