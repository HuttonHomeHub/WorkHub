Add the hours summary at `/hours/summary`: totals by week or by month for this month, last month, this year or any dates you choose (up to 366 days), with a totals row, warnings in words, and your leave year's allowance, used and remaining. The range and grouping are in the address, so a bookmark or a reload shows the same view, and "Download CSV" saves the rows shown in `h:mm` and decimal hours. This release also carries the week view's "This week" and "Balances" panels, including the switch that converts a week's excess to TOIL and overtime; they appear on the week view when it ships.
