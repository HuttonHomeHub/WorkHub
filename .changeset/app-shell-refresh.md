WorkHub has a new look: a calm, polished tool in the style of Linear or Stripe.

- **Everywhere:** set in Inter; a slate and indigo palette in light and dark; 32px controls; cards with hairline shadows; gentle motion (which your reduced-motion setting turns off); one focus ring; buttons show a spinner while they save.
- **Theme:** the header's Theme menu offers Light, Dark and System.
- **Week view:** fits beside its "This week" and "Balances" panels on a 1280px screen. Today's row is highlighted with a "Today" tag; flexi shows green when over and amber when under, always with "over" or "under"; a day's warnings, notes and bank holiday sit as tags under it; "This week" shows credited time against the target as a bar.
- **Summary:** weeks or months still under way are marked "In progress" or "Upcoming", with a note that their target counts days still to come; a break raised to the minimum shows as a note, not a warning; TOIL and overtime are grouped; the leave year is a card.
- **Settings:** each group is a card, with tidier tables and fields.
