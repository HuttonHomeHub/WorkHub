Add the hours tracker's week view: **Hours** is now in the sidebar and opens `/hours` on this week, with the week in the address so reload, back and bookmarks return to it. Type each day's start, end, break, leave and TOIL taken straight into the table (`0830`, `8:30`, `30m`, `7.5h` all work), press Enter to save a day or Esc to undo its changes; an end before the start is a night shift, marked "+1 day". Worked time, credited time, flexi and the week's totals update as you type, and warnings such as "Below 5:30 minimum" or "Before 07:00" appear on the day. Clear a day from its "⋯" menu (or by right-clicking the row) and undo it within 8 seconds, mark a bank holiday as worked, download the week as CSV, and jump back with "Go to today". Leaving with unsaved days asks first.
